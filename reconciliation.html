After an argument, a stretch of distance, or a breakup, the same questions keep circling. Tell us about the two of you and where things stand now. We’ll write an intuitive assessment of one relationship situation to help you understand the separation and think about reconnection or closure.

Inside your reading

We look at possible reasons for the separation, whether we read potential for reconciliation, the feelings and emotional blocks on each side, and what might support or delay reconnecting. The reading ends with suggested next steps, whether that means rebuilding or letting go. One reading covers one situation.
